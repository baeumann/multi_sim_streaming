Multi-SIM Stream

uplink visualiser

Reconstructs dead pixels from their closest live neighbours to hide the dropouts.

How it works

A — Quadrants: every SIM encodes one region of the frame. Losing a SIM blacks out a whole region of the broadcast.

B — Round-robin pixels: every SIM still encodes the whole frame, but only its own 1/n of the pixels survive assembly. Losing a SIM only leaves scattered dropouts, not dead regions.

Total failure: the server falls back to a direct fiber link to Twitch and keeps the stream alive with a “be right back” card instead of going dark.
